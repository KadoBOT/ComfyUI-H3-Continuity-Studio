A script, not a control panel

A film is a list of clips you read from top to bottom. You render a clip, watch it, and decide. When it's wrong, you say what you see and the Studio offers the fix.

The prototype is live. Nothing in it changes what the engine renders.

Prototype shows Renders are simulated and take a few seconds.

Decided

  • The word is “clip”. “Shot” keeps its H3 meaning: a timed stage inside one clip's prompt.
  • Deciding after each clip can be switched off. It is on for a new film. The switch is in the menu beside the Next button. Off, Next renders everything left and decisions wait for you.
  • There is no feature list. Features are reached from the script, the join, and “Something's not right”.

What changes in how it works

The user's questionTodayv1
Where do I start?An empty Timeline and a + Clip button.A short list of films that already work. The first one renders with nothing but model files.
What is my film?A strip of numbered boxes. The prompt sits in a narrow side panel, one box at a time.A script. Every clip's prompt is on the page, in order, next to its footage.
How do I make clip 2 follow clip 1?Add a clip, open Settings, and leave two checkboxes unticked.“What happens next?” has three answers: it keeps going, cut to a new angle, go somewhere new. A line down the margin shows where the film continues and where it breaks.
What do I do now?Choose between Render next, Render N remaining and Generate clip N.One button names the next step: write clip 4, render clip 4, watch clip 4 and decide, or fix how clip 3 starts.
Was that render good?Nothing asks. Takes are a list on the third tab.A finished clip waits for a decision: keep it or try again. Takes sit under the player as pictures.
It's wrong. How do I fix it?Know that memory, bridges, references, guide frames and seeds exist, and guess which applies.“Something's not right” lists what you might be seeing and offers the remedy that fits this clip and its join.
What will this change cost me?You find out afterwards.Before you act, the Studio says which later clips will need a new render and why.
Where are length, seed and frames?The Settings tab, as a form.A line of facts under the prompt. Click one to change it in place.
Where is the Timeline?Always on screen.The Time view, for work that needs a clock.

Added in this round

Open “A film in progress” and select a clip to try these. Each one is reached from the clip it belongs to.

FeatureWhere it lives now
Shots inside a clip“more” on a clip splits its prompt into Shot 1, Shot 2. They read as paragraphs of the same clip.
Soundscape and musicThe “sound” fact on a clip. What you write shows under the prompt as “We hear”.
Dialogue cuesThe “words” fact. Lines appear in the clip the way a script prints them: who, what, and whether they are on screen. Their timing is set in the Time view.
First and last frames, and stillsThe “pictures” fact: start or end on a picture from the film, or generate two candidates and pick one.
Several videos in one clip“more” adds a second video. It sits inside the clip with its own prompt and one sentence for the swap. If the next clip was set to continue, its join turns red, says why, and becomes the next step.
CastA row of people and places above the first clip. Selecting one shows their looks and where they appear. Once a film has a Cast, a cut can remember it.
RefModsFilm › Pictures and recordings, under the pictures.
PhoneThe player stays at the top with the decision under it, and the script scrolls beneath. Narrow this window to see it.
